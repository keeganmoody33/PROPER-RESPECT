# ADR-060: Data Export and Portability — The Right to Take Your Data

## Status
Accepted — 2026-06-05

## Context
Users own their data. GDPR and CCPA require data portability. But more importantly, it's the right thing to do. Users should be able to leave props with their full product history intact.

## Decision

### The Export Formats

| Format | Contents | Use Case |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **JSON** | Complete data structure (props, lineage, content, settings) | Migration to another platform, backup |
| **CSV** | Tabular data (props list, lineage list) | Spreadsheet analysis, reporting |
| **Markdown** | Human-readable profile (formatted text) | Blog post, README, personal website |
| **HTML** | Self-contained profile page (styled) | Standalone website, archive |
| **Linktree Import** | Linktree-compatible format | Migration to Linktree (if user leaves) |

### The Portability Promise

**Our commitment:**
- Export is always free (no paywall)
- Export is always available (no account lock-in)
- Export is complete (all data, not just some)
- Export is fast (generated in under 10 seconds)
- Export is private (download link expires in 24 hours)

**The "No Lock-in" Badge:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🔓 No Lock-In                         │
│                                     │
│ Your data is yours. Export anytime. │
│ Switch to another platform anytime.   │
│ We don't hold your data hostage.      │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Import Path (Reverse)

Users can also import their exported data back into PROPER-RESPECT:
- Re-import after account deletion (changed their mind)
- Import from another platform (if they support our format)
- Merge two accounts (personal + work)

## Consequences

### Positive
- Builds trust (users know they can leave)
- GDPR/CCPA compliance
- Enables migration (users can try props without risk)
- Markdown export is marketing (users share their stack)

### Negative
- Export feature makes it easier to leave
- Data export is compute-intensive (generating files)
- Storage of export files (temporary, but still cost)
- Users may export and never return

## Mitigations
- Export is a trust signal, not a churn driver
- Users who export are often power users (they stay)
- Export is fast and easy (reduces friction to try)
- No data deletion on export (account stays active)
