## Permission and privacy

Signing in identifies the person; it does not authorize mailbox access. Reading email requires separate, explicit source authorization. The email connector must be read-only: no send, compose, modify, or delete scopes, and no messages sent on the person's behalf. Revoking a source must stop future access. Source selection and revocation must be understandable in the product.

Raw evidence, original proposals, and review corrections remain private. Publication is a separate action, with cost visibility chosen per tool or in bulk. Approving a claim does not publish the evidence or subscribe the user to future refreshes.

## What a claim establishes

- Signup evidence establishes signup, not first use.
- A receipt establishes payment and its stated billing period, not activity.
- First observed use is the earliest use in available evidence, not necessarily the person's first use ever.
- Recent observed use is a dated event, not a claim that the person still uses the product.
- Usage totals retain their source, attribution scope, and measurement period. Missing periods stay unknown.
- Organization activity is not personal activity. Source authorization does not establish the truth of a derived claim.
- A successful tool/API call establishes that operation's result or access; usage requires a documented metric/event with a period, account scope, coverage and lineage. An agent's operation does not silently become human use.

Label source-reported data, assistant extractions, user entries, and inferences separately. Ask targeted questions only where evidence is missing, ambiguous, or disputed. Keep original suggestions alongside Correct / Incorrect / Incomplete / Unknown verdicts and corrections.
