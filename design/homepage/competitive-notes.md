# Field notes — what the neighbors do (Sep 2026)

Read live: linktr.ee, carrd.co, beacons.ai, about.me, plus the link-in-bio category writeups.

## The flows

**Linktree** — Hero: "A link in bio built for you" + 70M social proof + "Get started for free." Register page is ONE field (email) + Google/Apple OAuth. Handle comes immediately after. Templates and a marketplace do the "show don't tell." Paid unlocks: analytics, custom domain, remove branding.

**Carrd** — "Simple, free, fully responsive one-page sites for pretty much anything." CTA is "Choose a Starting Point" — the template gallery IS the homepage body. Pro is $19/**year**, an honesty flex that makes everyone else look extractive. Custom domains on paid.

**Beacons** — Creator-business suite: link page + store + email + media kit. Homepage is a wall of creator faces (social proof) + "Start for free." Sells the business, not the page.

**about.me** — The page IS the pitch: the hero demos a live profile, `about.me/bfeld` is shown as the object you get. Now pushing an "AI twin" angle.

## What all four share (table stakes we need)

1. **Handle-claim opens the funnel.** One input. The page you get is the pitch.
2. **"Free" said out loud** in the primary CTA.
3. **Live example profiles** you can click before signing up.
4. **Visible pricing** — even a simple two-tier.
5. **Custom domain as the paid unlock** (Linktree Pro, Carrd Pro, Beacons paid).

## What none of them do (our lane)

1. **Proof.** No competitor attaches evidence to a link or shows verification freshness.
2. **Lineage.** No "who put you on." No chain of custody for a recommendation.
3. **Per-product branded cards.** Their themes re-skin YOUR page; nobody renders each item in the product's own identity.
4. **A visitor action that isn't "click away."** "Take this card" turns a profile view into a signup with the lineage already attached — the referral loop is the product.
5. **Usage metering.** Cursor-blog-style usage graphs per tool. Nobody else can say "verified 2 days ago."

## Positioning sentence that survives contact

> Linktree lists links. Proper Respect proves them — what you use, how much, and who put you on.

## Homepage implications (implemented in v3)

- Handle strip directly under the hero (their register page, promoted to the landing).
- Branded cards section = our template gallery analog, but the gallery is four real product identities.
- Proof ledger = the freshness claim, compact.
- Pricing with the custom-domain tier ("Sovereign", props.yourdomain.com).
- "See the live example" links a real profile, not a marketing mock.
